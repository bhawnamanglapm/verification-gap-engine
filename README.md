# The Verification Gap: citation-verification research and platform design

**Research log plus architecture and wireframe documents. The clickable prototype and landing page described on the page are not in this repo.**

## In one minute

An ongoing research and design project on whether the citations in AI-assisted professional reports can be trusted. Part 1 is the research log (Q1 to Q25). Part 2 is a hands-on citation audit of a public 56-page report, which the page says found 51 of 100 in-text citations with no bibliography entry, followed by a verification framework, a platform architecture and wireframes. Part 3 adds a data-handling answer and a screen-completeness pass.
